# Proposal: other sources and product access

## Why

After the investment flows, the product needs bank/manual source support, explicit pipeline selection, exports or an access layer, and a storage/amendment design.

## What Changes

- Add planned bank/JPMPI/manual ingestion paths.
- Select among multiple available source pipelines explicitly.
- Plan product access, exports, storage, amendments, and tax-year configuration, including review checkpoints between source assembly and calculation.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `additional-income-sources`: ingest supported bank, statement, and manual inputs.
- `product-access-and-storage`: expose results and prepare future storage/amendment support.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

This later delivery remains broad and should be subdivided again when a specific source or interface is selected.
